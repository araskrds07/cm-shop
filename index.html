Help

Good questions

How is it made?

Every piece is printed when you order it, by our print partner Spreadshirt. Nothing sits in a warehouse.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your country. Spreadshirt lists it here: delivery costs and times.

Can I return it?

Yes, within 30 days. Details: returns.

Which size should I pick?

Hoodies and sweatshirts run regular. For the boxy, oversized look, take one size up. The exact size chart is on each product page in the shop.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs on Spreadshop, which shows the payment methods available in your country.

Where do you ship?

Spreadshirt ships to the UK, the EU and many other countries. Your country and delivery cost show up at checkout.

Something wrong with my order?

Spreadshirt prints and ships every order, so their team handles problems fastest: order help.

How do I wash it?

Inside out, 30°C, no tumble dryer, never iron over the print.
